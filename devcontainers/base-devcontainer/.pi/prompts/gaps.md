Review this session and identify my knowledge gaps. Optional topic filter: "${@:-none}".

Evidence to look for:
- Questions I asked that revealed a misunderstanding
- Wrong predictions or quiz answers
- Places I needed hints or said "just tell me"
- Assumptions you corrected
- Concepts I used loosely or mixed up

Rules:
- Base this only on what actually happened in this session. Don't invent gaps or pad the list.
- Be honest but constructive. If I showed no clear gaps, say so.
- Do not modify anything.

Format (concise):
- A list of at most 5 gaps, most important first. For each:
  - **Concept**, named so I can look it up
  - **Evidence**: the moment it showed up, in one line
  - **Next step**: one specific thing to read or practise, and a primary source where possible
- End by offering to run `/quiz` on the top gap, or to explain one of them.
